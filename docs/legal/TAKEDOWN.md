# Takedown requests

**Draft, not legal advice, needs review.**

DeCombat is an unofficial community project ([NOTICE.md](../../NOTICE.md)). Its repository is meant to hold no game files, and we
take reports that something belongs to someone else seriously. Contact: contact@decombat.com.

## What to send

1. Who you are, and your authority to act for the rights holder.
2. The work or mark you say is affected.
3. Exactly where the material is: repository path and commit, release file, pack name or server.
4. Why you believe its use isn't permitted.
5. How to reach you.
6. A statement that the information is accurate and, for copyright, made in good faith. A request under the US DMCA also needs
   what that law requires, including a signature and a statement under penalty of perjury.

Requests about material hosted on GitHub can also go directly to GitHub under its own process.

## What happens

We aim to acknowledge within 7 days. Where a request is clear and complete, we remove or disable the material while we look at
it. We tell the person who contributed it if we can, and they may respond. A counter-notice goes through the process the
applicable law sets.

## Mods and community content

Packs on the review list are removed by taking their row out of `community/vanilla_plus.json` and their zip off our servers. We
don't control other people's servers or repositories.

## Other things to report here

Copies of game files posted in an issue, a pull request or on our servers: tell us and we remove them, no formal request needed.
Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).
